Entry for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` writes exactly two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output`): line one warns the writer's past self about one
specific moment — the night he almost signed a $40,000 loan at the
kitchen table — and line two says in one sentence why that moment
mattered and what changed because of it. The output is fully
deterministic: 276 characters, two lines, byte-identical on every run.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
